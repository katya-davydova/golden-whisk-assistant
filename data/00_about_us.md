# Golden Whisk Patisserie — About Us

Golden Whisk Patisserie is a French-style patisserie and coffee shop in Willow Creek, Texas.
We bake French classics with a modern touch, using high-quality ingredients: European-style butter,
dark chocolate from Europe, real vanilla beans, and milk and cream from Texas dairy farms.

Everything is baked fresh every day in small batches. Because of this, popular items can sell out,
especially on weekends. Customers who want a specific item can order online in advance for pickup.

## What We Offer

- **Viennoiserie** — croissants, danishes, kouign-amann, and brioche, baked every morning.
- **Pastries and French desserts** — tarts, éclairs, macarons, and more, available all day.
- **Cakes** — ready-made whole cakes and custom cakes for celebrations.
- **Catering** — pastry platters and dessert boxes for offices, parties, and events.
- **Coffee bar** — espresso drinks made by our baristas.
- **Gift cards** — available in the shop.
- **Wholesale** — pastries for local cafés, restaurants, and businesses.

Customers can eat in the café or take their order to go.

## A Note From the Owner

Golden Whisk was founded by Chef Élise Moreau, a pastry chef trained in Lyon, France.
She opened the shop to bring real French pastry to the neighborhood and to be a warm, friendly
place where neighbors meet over coffee and a croissant.

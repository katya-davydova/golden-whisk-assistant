# Golden Whisk Patisserie — Allergens and Ingredients

## Important Safety Notice

All products at Golden Whisk Patisserie are made in one shared kitchen. The kitchen handles
wheat, milk, eggs, tree nuts (especially almonds and pistachios), peanuts, soy, and sesame.
Because of this, we cannot guarantee that any product is completely free of any allergen.

If a customer has a food allergy, they must always confirm with a staff member before buying.
Recipes and suppliers can change, and only staff can check the current ingredient labels.

| Tiramisu cake | wheat, milk, eggs, coffee; contains a small amount of alcohol (coffee liqueur) |

| Galette des rois (January) | wheat, milk, eggs, almonds; contains a small ceramic charm |
| Bûche de Noël (Christmas) | wheat, milk, eggs, soy; may contain a small amount of alcohol |

## Gluten-Free and Other Diets

- Macarons are made with almond flour and do not contain wheat flour, but they are made in a kitchen
  that uses wheat flour every day. They are not certified gluten-free.
- We do not currently offer vegan products.
- We do not currently offer sugar-free products.
- Custom cakes can sometimes be adjusted (for example, no nuts in the decoration). Such requests must be
  discussed with the cake decorator when placing a custom order. See the Custom Cake Orders document.

## Alcohol

The tiramisu cake contains coffee liqueur. Some seasonal desserts may also contain alcohol.
Customers who avoid alcohol should ask staff about seasonal items.

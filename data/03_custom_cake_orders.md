# Golden Whisk Patisserie — Custom Cake Orders

Custom cakes are made to order for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, and other events.

## How to Order

1. Place the order in person at the counter or by phone at (555) 014-2290.
2. Choose the size, cake flavor, filling, and decoration.
3. Pay a 50% non-refundable deposit to confirm the order.
4. Pay the remaining balance when picking up the cake.

Orders are not confirmed until the deposit is paid.

## Lead Time

| Order type | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| Standard custom cake (up to 10 inches) | 72 hours (3 days) |
| Tiered cake (2–3 tiers) | 7 days |
| Wedding cake | 4 weeks; tasting appointment recommended |
| Holiday weeks (Thanksgiving, Christmas, Valentine's Day) | 7 days for all custom cakes |

Rush orders with less notice may be possible for a $25 rush fee, only if the kitchen schedule allows.
Staff must confirm every rush order.

## Sizes and Starting Prices

| Size | Servings | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| 6-inch round | 6–8 | $55 |
| 8-inch round | 10–14 | $75 |
| 10-inch round | 20–24 | $110 |
| Quarter sheet | 20–25 | $95 |
| Two-tier (6 + 8 inch) | 25–30 | $180 |

Prices go up for fondant, sugar flowers, gold leaf, or detailed hand-painted designs.
The final price is confirmed by the cake decorator.

## Flavors and Fillings

Cake flavors: vanilla, chocolate, lemon, red velvet, carrot.
Fillings: vanilla custard, chocolate ganache, raspberry, salted caramel, fresh strawberries
(strawberries available March to June only).

## Writing and Decorations

- Writing on the cake (up to 30 characters) is free.
- Photo prints on edible paper cost $12.
- We do not copy copyrighted characters or logos (for example, cartoon characters or sports team logos).
  The decorator can suggest an original design in a similar style.

## Wedding Cake Tastings

Tastings cost $35 per appointment (up to 2 people). The tasting fee is subtracted from the final
price if the couple orders their wedding cake with us. Tastings are on Tuesdays and Wednesdays only.

## Cake Delivery

- Delivery is available for tiered cakes and wedding cakes only, within 15 miles of the shop.
- Delivery fee: $20 within 5 miles, $35 for 5–15 miles.
- Wedding cake delivery with setup at the venue costs $75 and must be booked when ordering.
- All other custom cakes are picked up at the shop.

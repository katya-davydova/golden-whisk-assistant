# Golden Whisk Patisserie — Hours, Online Orders, and Pickup

## Location and Contact

Address: 210 Maple Street, Suite 4, Willow Creek, TX (in the Maple Commons shopping center)
Phone: (555) 014-2290
Parking: free parking in the shopping center lot.

## Opening Hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | Closed |
| Tuesday – Saturday | 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM |
| Sunday | 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM |

Holiday hours:
- Thanksgiving Day: closed.
- Day before Thanksgiving: open 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM; pre-order pickup only after 2:00 PM.
- Christmas Eve: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM.
- Christmas Day and New Year's Day: closed.

## Ordering Online

- Customers can order pastries, macaron boxes, and ready-made whole cakes on the shop's website
  for in-store pickup.
- Online orders must be placed by 8:00 PM the day before pickup.
- Online orders are paid in full when ordering.
- Custom cakes cannot be ordered online; see the Custom Cake Orders document.

## Pickup

- All online orders and custom cakes are picked up at the counter during opening hours.
- Customers choose a pickup time window when they order.
- Orders not picked up by closing time on the pickup day will be held until the next opening day,
  but freshness cannot be guaranteed.

## Delivery

- The shop does not deliver regular orders, individual pastries, or drinks.
- We are not on food delivery apps.
- Delivery is available only for catering orders and tiered or wedding cakes.
  See the Catering and Wholesale document and the Custom Cake Orders document for details.

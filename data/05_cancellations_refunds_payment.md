# Golden Whisk Patisserie — Payment, Cancellations, and Refunds

## Payment Methods

We accept cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
We do not accept personal checks.

## Deposits for Custom Orders

- Custom cakes require a 50% deposit when ordering.
- The deposit is non-refundable, because ingredients are bought and work is planned for that order.
- The remaining balance is paid at pickup or before delivery.

## Changes to an Order

- Changes to flavor, size, or design can be made up to 48 hours before pickup at no charge.
- Changes less than 48 hours before pickup are possible only if staff confirm the kitchen can do them.
- Changes to wedding cakes must be made at least 14 days before the event.

## Cancellations

| When the customer cancels | What happens |
|---|---|
| More than 72 hours before pickup | Deposit can be moved to a new date within 60 days |
| Less than 72 hours before pickup | Deposit is kept; no refund |
| Wedding cake, more than 30 days before event | Deposit can be moved to a new date within 6 months |
| Wedding cake, less than 30 days before event | Deposit is kept; no refund |

## Problems With a Product

If a customer is not happy with a product, they should contact the shop on the same day,
with the product or a photo of it and the receipt. A manager will review each case and may offer
a replacement or store credit. Cash refunds are decided by the manager only.

The shop is not responsible for damage to cakes after pickup (for example, during transport in a hot car).
See the Storage and Care document for transport tips.

## Gift Cards

Gift cards are sold at the counter in any amount from $10 to $200. Gift cards do not expire
and cannot be exchanged for cash.

# Golden Whisk Patisserie — Storage, Transport, and Freshness

## How Long Products Stay Fresh

| Product | Best within | How to store |
|---|---|---|
| Croissants and pain au chocolat | Same day | Room temperature, in the paper bag |
| Fruit tarts, éclairs, mille-feuille | 1 day | Refrigerator |
| Macarons | 5 days | Refrigerator in a closed box; take out 20 minutes before eating |
| Mousse and tiramisu cakes | 2 days | Refrigerator |
| Carrot cake | 3 days | Refrigerator |
| Custom cakes with buttercream | 3 days | Refrigerator; take out 1 hour before serving |

Croissants can be warmed in an oven at 350°F for 3–4 minutes the next day.
Do not microwave croissants.

## Freezing

- Croissants and macarons can be frozen for up to 1 month in an airtight container.
- We do not recommend freezing fruit tarts, éclairs, or mousse cakes.

## Transporting Cakes

- Carry the cake box flat, on the floor of the car or in the trunk, not on a seat.
- Keep the car cool with air conditioning, especially in Texas summer. Do not leave a cake in a parked car.
- Tiered cakes are heavy and fragile; we recommend our delivery service for cakes with 2 or more tiers.

## Outdoor Events

Buttercream and mousse cakes melt quickly in heat. For outdoor events, keep the cake in the shade
and serve it within 1 hour of taking it out of the refrigerator. Fondant cakes handle heat a little better.

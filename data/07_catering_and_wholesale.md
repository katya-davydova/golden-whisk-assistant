# Golden Whisk Patisserie — Catering and Wholesale

## Catering

We provide pastries and desserts for office meetings, parties, showers, and other events.

### Catering Options

| Option | What is included | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Morning box (serves 10–12) | 12 mini croissants, 6 mini danishes, 6 mini brioche | $65 |
| Dessert platter (serves 15–20) | 30 mini pastries: tartlets, éclairs, macarons | $110 |
| Macaron tower | 60 macarons on a display stand, up to 3 flavors | $175 |
| Coffee carafe (serves 10) | Hot drip coffee, cups, cream, and sugar | $35 |

### How to Order Catering

- Catering orders are placed by phone at (555) 014-2290 or in person at the counter.
- Minimum notice: 48 hours. For orders over $300, at least 5 days.
- A 50% deposit is required to confirm catering orders over $150.

### Catering Delivery

- Delivery is available for catering orders over $75, Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- Delivery area: within 15 miles of the shop.
- Delivery fee: $20 within 5 miles, $35 for 5–15 miles.

## Wholesale (Partner With Us)

Golden Whisk supplies pastries to local cafés, restaurants, hotels, and offices.

- Wholesale products: croissants, pain au chocolat, danishes, and macarons.
- Minimum wholesale order: $150 per delivery.
- Wholesale customers need a short application and a regular weekly schedule.
- To become a partner, businesses should contact the shop manager by phone.

The chatbot cannot give wholesale prices. Wholesale pricing is discussed directly with the manager.

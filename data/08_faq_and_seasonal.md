# Golden Whisk Patisserie — Frequently Asked Questions and Seasonal Specials

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you have seating?**
Yes. The café has 8 small tables inside and 3 tables on the patio. Seating is first come, first served.
We do not take table reservations.

**Do you have Wi-Fi?**
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for customers. Ask staff for the password.

**Are pets allowed?**
Only service animals are allowed inside. Dogs on a leash are welcome on the patio.

**Can I bring my own design or photo for a custom cake?**
Yes. Customers can show a photo or sketch as inspiration. The decorator will create a similar original
design. We do not copy copyrighted characters or logos.

**Do you make cakes for children's birthdays?**
Yes. Popular choices are vanilla or chocolate cake with buttercream, colorful decorations, and
the child's name and age written on top. Candles can be added for $2 per pack.

**Do you have nut-free products?**
No product can be guaranteed nut-free, because nuts (especially almonds) are used every day in our kitchen.
Customers with nut allergies must speak with a staff member before ordering.

**Do you have a loyalty program?**
Yes. Customers get one stamp per visit with a purchase of $5 or more. After 10 stamps, they receive
a free pastry of their choice (up to $7). Ask for a loyalty card at the counter.

**Do you offer corporate gifts?**
Yes. Macaron gift boxes (6, 12, or 24 pieces) can be ordered with a custom ribbon and card for
orders of 10 boxes or more. Minimum notice is 5 days. Order by phone.

**Can I order a cake for the same day?**
Ready-made whole cakes from the display case can be bought the same day while available.
Custom cakes need at least 72 hours' notice. See the Custom Cake Orders document.

**Do you sell bread?**
We sell brioche loaves ($9) and baguettes ($4) on Saturdays and Sundays only, while supplies last.

**Are you hiring?**
Job openings are posted in the shop and on our website. Applicants can also leave a resume at the counter.

## Seasonal and Holiday Pre-Orders

Holiday items must be pre-ordered. Pre-orders open about 3 weeks before each holiday and close when
the kitchen is full or on the deadline below, whichever comes first.

| Holiday | Special items | Pre-order deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving | Pumpkin tart ($32), pecan tart ($34), apple tarte tatin ($30) | Sunday before Thanksgiving |
| Christmas | Bûche de Noël (yule log cake, $48 for 8 servings), holiday macaron box ($30) | December 20 |
| New Year / Epiphany | Galette des rois (king cake with almond cream, $36) — available in January | 2 days before pickup |
| Valentine's Day | Heart-shaped raspberry mousse cake ($38), red velvet macarons | February 10 |
| Mother's Day | Floral fruit tart ($40), flower-decorated macaron box ($32) | Wednesday before Mother's Day |

Holiday pre-orders are paid in full when ordering and are picked up at the shop on the chosen date.
Holiday pre-orders cannot be cancelled less than 72 hours before pickup.

The galette des rois has a small ceramic charm (fève) baked inside. Customers should warn guests,
especially children, before eating.
